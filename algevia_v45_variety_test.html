How this test chooses questions

It moves through different questions matching your area/topic/grade selection, without repeating a question until the selection is exhausted. It does not advance difficulty based on correctness, because V45 lacks multiple questions of the same method. A later production engine must use pools of varied questions per method, prerequisite relationships, grade ceilings, and mastery evidence.
