Algevia Education

Learn your way. Improve your way.

From 11+ to A-Level, Algevia helps students understand what they know, identify what they need to improve and practise at their own pace — with or without a tutor.

11+ → GCSE → A-Level

Maths • Physics • Chemistry

📚 Learn by yourself

Take a free diagnostic, discover your strengths and weaknesses, practise at your level and receive intelligent feedback to help you improve.

🎯 Match My Tutor

When you want additional support, Algevia can match you with a tutor based on your needs, ability, target grade, subject, budget and preferences.

🤝 Learn with both

Combine expert tutoring with personalised Algevia practice between lessons. Your learning profile continues to adapt as you progress.

🧠 Free Diagnostic

Find out what you know, where you need help and what you should work on next.

The diagnostic can help establish a starting level, identify topic weaknesses and guide your personalised learning pathway.

✏️ Intelligent Practice Papers

Choose your stage, subject, exam board, tier where applicable, target grade, topics, paper type, number of questions and timing.

Or choose: Let Algevia choose — Algevia analyses your previous performance and recommends what you should practise next.

Questions are designed around your selected level, target grade, topics and learning needs. They are intended to test understanding, reasoning and common misconceptions — not simply repeat questions.

After you complete a paper, Algevia analyses your answers, explains what went wrong and recommends your next step.
