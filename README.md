# ALGEVIA

## Connect. Learn. Get things done.

Algevia is an AI-powered marketplace connecting people with trusted independent providers.

## Our Mission

Help people find the right person for what they need.

## How Algevia Works

1. Customer tells Algevia what they need
2. Algevia understands the requirement
3. AI identifies suitable providers
4. Customer chooses a provider
5. Customer books and pays through the platform
6. Independent provider delivers the service
7. Customer leaves a review
8. Algevia improves future matching

## Initial Launch

### Algevia Education

Starting with GCSE Maths tutoring.

## Future Verticals

- Education
- Trades
- Wellness
- Jobs
- Other trusted services

## Business Model

Algevia is a marketplace platform.

Providers are independent and are not direct employees of Algevia.

Algevia earns revenue by facilitating connections, bookings and services between customers and independent providers.

## Vision

Build one trusted platform where people can find the right person for almost any service, skill or opportunity they need.
